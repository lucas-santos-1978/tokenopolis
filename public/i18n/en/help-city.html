🔎 On the building

  • Yellow top with scaffolding: floors added this week
  • 🏗️Crane: used Claude in the last 15 minutes
  • 🎈Balloons: first week on the map
  • 🚁Helipad: the biggest build of the week in the city (golden: in the region)
  • 🌿Rooftop garden: 5 or more workdays in a row (on houses, a flower bed)
  • 📡Lit antenna: used today (blinking green: using it now)
  • 🦉Pink neon: used it late at night (10 pm to 5 am) on 2 of the last 7 days
  • 🚩Golden flag: builds in 2 cities · 👑 crown: 3 or more
  • 💎Black and gold banner: a Mega Builder ({megaFloors} floors added up in 2 or more cities); tap the building and the card takes you to the campus
  • 🏅Token medal: a person’s tokens added up across every city earn a medal at each mark: 100M (bronze), 250M (silver), 500M (gold), 1B (platinum), 2B (sapphire), 5B (emerald) and 10B (cosmic). It shows on the card and the pictures; “🏅 Medal” downloads the medal alone, with or without the name, ready to print. Passing a mark brings a notice on screen, a line in 🔔 and a note in the newspaper
  • 🥋Black pagoda: Dojo black belt (black lacquer, eaves, lanterns and a golden tip; the height is still the floors). Below 15 floors, a dark wooden house; the giants keep their shape, with the palette and a pagoda at the base
  • 🔥On fire: more than {idleFire} days without Claude (soot on the walls, flames on the roof and out of the windows, a column of smoke)
  • 🧱Rubble: more than {idleRubble} days without Claude (a heap of debris, a broken wall and twisted beams). Using Claude again puts the building back up the same day
  • 📍YOU: the building you marked as yours
  • ⭕Pulsing ring: the countdown milestone

🎡 Monuments

  • ✈️Airport: become a Megalopolis
  • 🏟️Stadium: 60% of the city building this week
  • 🎡Ferris wheel: grow 10% this week
  • 🌳Central Park: 50 buildings
  • 📺TV Tower: have a skyscraper
  • 🗽Leadership Statue: lead the week
  • ⛲Fountain Square: 3 newcomers this week
  • 🗿Obelisk: 10 billion tokens
  • 🎩Mayor of the month: whoever built the most in the city last month, with a statue and, in front of it, a rally where the mayor speaks to the crowd
  • 🥋City Dojo: appears with the team’s first belt (white included) and grows with every belt: Dojo (1), Temple (3) and Great Temple (7). Tap it to see the masters (black belts) and, only there, who is on the way (white, blue, purple, brown)

The weekly ones go back to zero on Monday. Hover any monument to see what it is and what earns it. Every belt counts for the Dojo; only the black one changes the building.

🛣️ In the region

  • 🪧City sign: the milestone, the total floors and the buildings; click to visit
  • 🟡Golden road: links the cities that share the most people; the golden cars are those people
  • 🚩"6 in common": how many people the two cities share
  • 🚗Traffic: the highways and the streets · 🚶 sidewalks: sessions in the last hour
  • 💎Corporate District: across the channel, behind the cities: a campus for each Mega Builder. Each wing is one of their cities and rises 1 floor for every 10 floors there, with the city’s name at the door; the diamond crowns the tallest wing. Tap a campus for its card: golden lines run along the streets from each of the person’s buildings to it
  • 🚧Foundation: the next in line (2 or more cities and half the line or more), with a board showing how far they are

🏆 Panels

  • 🏗️Build of the week: most floors added this week
  • 🏙️Biggest metropolis: most floors in total
  • 💡Liveliest city: % of the people who built this week
  • 🚀Growth: this week’s floors over what the city already had
  • ⚔️Derby: the two cities closest in total, and the score of the week
  • 💎Mega Builders: who builds in 2 or more cities and has added up {megaFloors} floors, and who is next in line; tap a name to fly to the campus
  • ⏳Countdown: milestones expected to fall within a week, at the current pace

🌗 Sky and live

  • 🌅Day and night follow the time in {zone}; the sun and the shadows move with the hour
  • 🪟Windows at night: the building’s height stands for the 24 hours (from the bottom, early morning, up to the night); lit = used Claude in that hour
  • ✨"+1 floor": just added a whole floor
  • 🎆Fireworks: a milestone, the lead, a monument or a new mayor
  • 🚁The helicopter circles the tallest building in view; the airship carries the news

📍 Your building

  • 📍"This is my building" on the card: a pin, the 📍 button, a notice when it grows, and your mark on the climb
  • 📈The race: your place in the week, the floors to pass whoever is just above, last week against this one
  • 🕒Habits: the hours of the day you build and the models you use (last 7 days)
  • 📸Card: a square image of the building (or the campus) to share · 🏙️ Rooftop view: the city from its roof · 🏔️ the same person on the climb
  • 🎉Back from the ashes: balloons and confetti on the day a burnt building is used again · 🏊 a pool on the day of a personal record · 🌸 a zen garden in the week of a new belt
  • 🎯Mission of the week: the whole region against last week's floors plus 10%, under this week's builds

🖱️ How to use

  • 🔄Drag to rotate, mouse wheel to zoom, right button to pan
  • 👆Click a sign, a trophy, a ranking row or a building to open the city; Esc or ‹ goes back
  • 🏢Mouse over a building: who it is. Click: the details, with the timeline
  • 🧰🔍 find a building or a city (/) · 🗺️ data maps (🚒 health: days without Claude) · 📰 newspaper · 🏆 overall ranking (everyone, every city added up) · 🎬 history · 🌗 time of day · 🔔 notices and the week’s diary
  • 👁️Hides panels and names; P hides them all · G spins on its own · ? opens this help
  • 📺On a TV: ?tour alternates the region and the cities with the camera spinning. The tab builds the link and lists everything the URL accepts
