# Roadmap

Ideas that are not built yet, roughly by area. Effort: S (small), M (medium), L (large). Open an
issue if you want to pick one up or argue for another.

## Sharing

- **Time-lapse of the day** as a downloadable video (15–20 s): the stage since midnight sped up,
  with the clock, the overtakes and the sky changing. Needs hourly usage since midnight. (M)
- **Weekly recap** on Sunday night: champion, biggest single-day climb, the night owl, the most
  consistent, the biggest mover. (M–L)

## Epic moments on screen

- **Ghost of yesterday's winner** climbing at the pace the champion had at the same hour. (M)
- **Celebrations**: a planted flag and fireworks for whoever passes the day's mark, a camera dive
  with an "OVERTAKE" banner in the top 5, a spotlight on a new leader. (S–M)
- **Collectible gear**: a golden helmet after 3 stage wins, a cape for the week's champion, a record
  backpack, shown on the climber and on the card. (M)
- **Graduation moments** for the belt program: a mark on the building timeline, news/blimp/fireworks
  on the day, the belt on the climber. (S–M)
- **Real weather**: rain when the data source is down, a rainbow when the region sets a record. (S)

## For the office TV

- **Cinematic TV mode** (the tour already has a caption): drone shots, close-ups of whoever is climbing, letterbox bars and optional
  sounds (wind, carabiners, a crowd on overtakes). `?tour` is the base. (M)
- **Seasonal themes**: Halloween (pumpkins at the camps, bats, fog), snow for the holidays. (S)
- **Optional sounds** in the city (off by default): traffic, a chime on a new floor, fireworks, the
  monster's roar. (M)

## The city

- **Ready-made CC0 kits** (Kenney's city kits) instead of generated pieces, as an option. (M)
- **A 3D sign at the Dojo entrance** with the city name and the belt count. (S)

## Small things

- A discreet "1 hour ago" mark on the climb's ruler.
- Spread the avatars piled at the base of the mountain at the start of the week.
- A `data.json` exporter example (from a CSV) next to the file source.
